Detailed Description

Typed matrices over the compile-time sparsity pattern storage of the Sparse Linear Algebra library.

The storage, its patterns, and its operations are those of https://github.com/FrancoisCarouge/SparseLinearAlgebra. This plug-in only names the typed matrices over it and constructs them from typed values. It is included before the core, so that the core's qualified calls find its norms.
