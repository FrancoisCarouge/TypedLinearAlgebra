◆ storage

using fcarouge::benchmark::storage

The entry points of the operations, each linked alone, of the typed matrix and of its freestanding storage, named after their variant and operation.

The operands are the caller's. A matrix result is statically allocated, a scalar result returned. The equality has no storage operation: the library implements it over the typed elements.
